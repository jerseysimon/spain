# All-inclusive / family club options — France vs Spain

*A shortlist for a couple of nights off the campervan on the Mediterranean, for a family of four (kids aged 7 and 10). Google ratings noted as of July 2026 — they drift over time, so re-check when booking.*

**The headline:** true all-inclusive (meals *and* drinks bundled) is rare in France — the French equivalent is the *club* model (half- or full-board + kids' clubs + animations + pools, buffet breakfasts in summer). Spain does proper all-inclusive far more cheaply and abundantly, and the Costa Brava starts about an hour south of Collioure. The trade-off in Spain is proximity vs scale: the closest border towns are lovely low-key beach hotels, while the big all-inclusive-with-waterslides resorts sit a bit further south.

## 🇫🇷 France — top 3

### FR1 · Belambra Clubs "Les Ayguades", Gruissan
- **Google:** 3.3 ★ (628 reviews)
- **Location:** Av. de la Jonque, 11430 Gruissan (Aude) — beachfront, ~1 hr from Collioure, ~45 min from Carcassonne
- **Format:** Apartments with kitchenette; "tout compris" is a half- or full-board formule with buffets, daily animations and kids'/teens' clubs (ages 3–17); buffet breakfast in summer
- **Best for:** Fitting neatly into your route — beach days that step straight onto the drive north
- **What guests say (paraphrased):** An active family club on a big sandy beach with good kids' entertainment; several reviewers feel the site is dated and short on polish, which is reflected in the middling score
- **Heads-up:** Kids' clubs run during French school holidays only (your late-August dates fall inside them — confirm the end date); holiday-village feel rather than hotel
- **Contact:** +33 4 68 75 17 17 · belambra.fr

### FR2 · Belambra Clubs "Les Salins", Port Camargue
- **Google:** 4.0 ★ (1,030 reviews)
- **Location:** 280 Rue Amiot d'Inville, 30240 Le Grau-du-Roi (Gard) — ~1.5 hr east of Collioure, near Aigues-Mortes, the Pont du Gard and Nîmes
- **Format:** Same Belambra club model, premium tier; brand new (2022) with three pools
- **Best for:** The best-rated French pick — pairs well with a Rhône-valley route north (Pont du Gard as a bonus) rather than the Carcassonne/Millau line
- **What guests say (paraphrased):** Clean and modern with excellent, well-kept pools and shallow, kid-friendly beach water; a few find the rooms small for the premium price and recommend bringing mosquito repellent
- **Heads-up:** Pricier tier; housekeeping tends to be weekly; it's a detour east from your drawn route
- **Contact:** +33 4 66 80 00 10 · belambra.fr

### FR3 · Club Med Opio en Provence
- **Google:** 4.1 ★ (2,620 reviews)
- **Location:** Chem. Cambarnier N, 06650 Opio (near Grasse, inland Provence) — a big detour east, only sensible if the trip finishes on the Côte d'Azur
- **Format:** The genuine all-inclusive — meals, drinks, shows, flying trapeze, and kids'/teens' clubs from 4 months to 17 years
- **Best for:** Families who want the full bells-and-whistles all-inclusive and don't mind reshaping the route
- **What guests say (paraphrased):** A stunning setting with praised food and activities and kids who love the clubs; a recurring minority flag small rooms and occasional overbooking
- **Heads-up:** Well off your northward route; the brand premium comes at a price
- **Contact:** +33 4 93 09 71 00 · clubmed.com

*Also considered: Belambra "Les Rives de Thau", Balaruc-les-Bains — 3.8 ★ (1,499). On the Thau lagoon near Sète, spa-town setting rather than open beach.*

## 🇪🇸 Spain — top 3 (Costa Brava)

### ES1 · Cala Montjoi, Roses
- **Google:** 4.4 ★ (3,784 reviews)
- **Location:** Carrer la Roca, 17480 Cala Montjoi, Roses (Girona) — ~1 hr south of Collioure, inside the Cap de Creus natural park
- **Format:** A holiday village in a cove — buffet dining, kids' clubs, its own diving school, hiking straight from the door
- **Best for:** Your outdoorsy family — coves, walking trails and watersports, with the legendary elBulli site hidden in the same bay
- **What guests say (paraphrased):** Families return year after year for the safe, beautiful setting between the hills and the sea, with a well-liked buffet; a few note the cove is rocky rather than sandy and the water runs cooler this far north
- **Heads-up:** Not a big sandy beach; more nature-retreat than resort
- **Contact:** +34 972 25 62 12 · montjoi.com

### ES2 · Hotel Spa Mediterráneo Park, Roses
- **Google:** 4.5 ★ (2,302 reviews)
- **Location:** Carrer de Francisco Quevedo 15, 17480 Roses (Girona) — near Santa Margarida beach and the Aquabrava water park
- **Format:** A proper hotel with three pools, a shin-deep kids' pool with a slide, a kids' club running late, and a buffet
- **Best for:** A comfortable, well-kept hotel base close to the border with plenty of pool space
- **What guests say (paraphrased):** Spotless, spacious and with a good buffet and helpful staff; some note the crowd skews older and quieter, and the "all-inclusive" really only covers drinks at breakfast — lunch, dinner and bar drinks are extra
- **Heads-up:** Check exactly what the board/AI package includes before booking
- **Contact:** +34 972 25 63 00 · hotelsmediterraneo.com

### ES3 · Golden Bahía de Tossa & Spa, Tossa de Mar
- **Google:** 4.5 ★ (3,436 reviews)
- **Location:** Av. Puerto Rico 29, 17320 Tossa de Mar (Girona) — ~2.5 hr south of the border
- **Format:** Genuine all-inclusive with pirate-ship waterslides, a big entertainment team and a spa — set in the walled medieval town of Tossa (a castle right on the beach, not a church)
- **Best for:** The full all-inclusive-with-slides experience *and* a genuinely lovely town — the best of both worlds
- **What guests say (paraphrased):** Consistently praised for its hard-working entertainment team, cleanliness and spacious, relaxed feel, about ten minutes' walk from the beach; a couple mention short beds and weak Wi-Fi
- **Heads-up:** The furthest south of these picks, so the biggest dip off-route
- **Contact:** +34 972 34 31 30 · goldenhotels.com

*Also considered: Hotel Panorama, L'Estartit — 4.3 ★ (2,148), superb for Medes Islands snorkelling and an in-house dive centre; Rosamar Garden Resort, Lloret de Mar — 4.0 ★ (3,712), cheap full-AI with a splash park but in the busy party town.*

## Booking notes
- **Mid-August is peak** — the busiest fortnight in Europe. Book early and check minimum-stay rules; some clubs prefer week-long bookings in high summer, which can make a two-night stay tricky.
- **French clubs:** kids'-club operation is tied to French school holidays; your dates should qualify, but confirm the end date.
- **Routing:** the Costa Brava sits below Aigüestortes, so a Spanish resort can slot in after the mountains without backtracking — and could replace the Collioure/Argelès leg entirely if you'd rather spend the beach days on the cheaper Spanish side.
- Ratings and review counts above are Google, captured July 2026.
