The itinerary

Six legs, stop by stop

Each block is a base we settle into for a few nights. Nights are a starting point, not gospel — we can move a day between legs once we see how the kids are travelling.

1

Basque Country

15–17 Aug · 2 nightsLP p379Sea & food

Ferry docks Bilbao · San Sebastián ~1 hr along the coast

What we'll do

Half a day in Bilbao for the Guggenheim — the building and the giant flower-dog "Puppy" outside. Then La Concha beach and the creaky old funicular fairground up Monte Igueldo in San Sebastián.

For the kids

The clifftop fairground, gentle surf at Zurriola, and a pintxos crawl where you order one, eat, move on — dinner as a treasure hunt.

Good food

San Sebastián is the guidebook's number-two food pick — pintxos through the Parte Vieja, chilled txakoli, and whole turbot grilled over coals down the coast at Getaria.

Where to stay

Campsites along the Basque coast between the two cities; book ahead for mid-August.

Optional detour — La Rioja (LP p398). Spain's premier wine country sits just south of here, roughly on the way toward the mountains. Bodega-hopping with architecture as striking as the wine — one lazy afternoon's worth if we fancy it.

2

Ordesa y Monte Perdido

17–20 Aug · 3 nightsLP p340Mountains

~3.5–4 hr drive down to Torla · base in the valley

What we'll do

The Ordesa valley walk toward the Cola de Caballo waterfall, past the Gradas de Soaso cascades. The flat lower river section is glorious and we turn back whenever we've had enough.

Hut-to-hut

Refugio de Góriz is the classic overnight — but it's ~800 m of climbing, a real stretch for a 7-year-old. See the decisions below.

Van logistics

In August no private vehicles into the valley — park at Torla and take the shuttle bus to the trailhead.

Where to stay

Camping Río Ara or Camping Ordesa around Torla / Broto. Ternasco (mountain lamb) for dinner.

3

Aigüestortes & Sant Maurici

20–22 Aug · 2 nightsLP p308Mountains

~3 hr of gorgeous mountain driving east · base at Espot

What we'll do

A 4×4 jeep-taxi from Espot drops us minutes from the Estany de Sant Maurici, with the twin Encantats peaks behind it. Easy loops around the lake for all four of us.

For the kids

The jeep-taxi ride is half the fun, and the cool mountain air is a blessing if the plains are baking.

Bigger walks

This is the home of the Carros de Foc refuge circuit — proper hut-to-hut country to file away for a future trip.

Where to stay

Campsites around Espot; same vehicle rules — you walk or jeep into the park core. Val d'Aran (LP p310) is next door — parked as a winter-ski idea, not this trip.

4

French Mediterranean

22–26 Aug · 4 nightsBeach & unwind

~2.5–3 hr over the border to the coast · base near Collioure / Argelès

What we'll do

Collioure is the payoff — a jewel of a Catalan seaside town with sheltered coves, a waterfront castle (a fortress, not a church) and anchovies everywhere. A boat trip along the Côte Vermeille.

For the kids

Long sandy beaches at Argelès, one of Europe's biggest and most family-geared camping stretches. Beach mornings, siesta, late dinners.

Good food

Seafood and Collioure anchovies; a swim and a taste of sweet Banyuls wine down the coast.

Where to stay

Big family campsites around Argelès-sur-Mer — the busiest to book, so lock it in early.

Just over the border — the Costa Brava. The Catalan coves you logged sit right below Collioure. Easy to fold in a day on the Spanish side, or base there instead if we'd rather have Spanish prices and quieter beaches.

5

The run north

26–29 Aug · 3 nights en routeOn the road

~8–9 hr to Paris, broken over two easy days

Carcassonne

A full-on fairytale walled citadel about 1½ hr from the coast — the kind of castle the kids will remember.

Millau viaduct

The tallest bridge in the world. Stop at the viewpoint below first, then drive across the top.

For the kids

The Tarn gorges below Millau are made for swimming and canoeing — a good place to break the drive.

Where to stay

A night around Millau, then a second hop up through Burgundy or the Loire to within an hour of Disney.

6

Disneyland & home

29–31 Aug · 2 nightsBig finish

Park near Marne-la-Vallée · ferry from Calais Monday 31st

What we'll do

A full day at Disneyland Paris on the 30th — the treat that carries us through the long drive north.

Getting home

Roughly 3 hr from Disney to Calais on the 31st for the crossing back.

Where to stay

A pitch near the parks for the night before, so we're at the gates when they open.

Worth deciding

One day at Disney, or two? See the decisions below.

Let's talk it through

Decisions to make together

The plan holds together as-is, but these are the real forks in the road. Worth a chat over dinner before anything's booked.

1

Picos de Europa — in or out? LP p412

The guidebook's top outdoor pick — kayaking down the Río Sella and walking past high mountain lakes, right up our street. But it's ~2.5 hr west of Bilbao, the wrong way for a Pyrenees-then-Med trip. Skip it this time, or swap it in and give up a chunk of the French Med?

2

Both mountain parks, or just Ordesa?

Two back-to-back mountain moves is the most tiring stretch with the kids. Ordesa is the more jaw-dropping of the two if we want to cut one and slow down.

3

A real hut night, or day-walks with a bail-out?

Sleeping in a mountain refuge is a brilliant idea and a big ask for a 7-year-old. Safer bet: day-hikes to a refuge and back, with the overnight as a "if the weather's perfect" bonus.

4

How many pure beach days?

Four nights on the Med gives real downtime after the mountains. Trim to three if we'd rather have a slower drive north.

5

Disney: one day or two?

One day fits the schedule; a second means cutting a night somewhere earlier. Depends how much the kids build it up.

Practicalities

Before we book

Mid-August is the busiest fortnight in Europe — the good pitches and the ferries go early. Tap an item to tick it off (just for this view; it won't save).

  • Book campsites for every leg now, especially Argelès and the Disney pitch
  • Confirm 2026 vehicle-ban dates and shuttle / jeep times for Ordesa and Aigüestortes
  • Reserve the Bilbao ferry out and the Calais crossing home
  • Buy Disneyland tickets and a pitch near Marne-la-Vallée for the 29th
  • Sort French autoroute tolls — a télépéage tag saves queuing
  • Pack for two climates: mountain layers and full beach kit
  • Download offline maps and a van-stop app (park4night / France Passion)
  • Check the van kit: gas, levelling ramps, EHU adapter, water hose
